    Why open-weight AI?

    • Local inference: Gemma can run on your machine through Ollama, so quest personalization does not require a closed commercial chat API.
    • Control & replaceability: You can inspect the stack, adjust prompts, and swap OLLAMA_MODEL for another compatible open-weight model (subject to each model's license).
    • Prompt privacy (local only): During local inference, prompts sent to Ollama stay on your device. SerpApi still receives location queries, and MongoDB may store quests if configured.
    • Open innovation: Open-weight models let builders experiment without tying the product to one vendor's inference API.

    TrailQuest still needs internet for real location search (SerpApi). That is separate from where the language model runs.
